PROVENANCE AND FALLBACKS
The APK includes icons/bundled-sources.json with every original source path,
pinned revision, bundled asset path, SHA-256 hash, exact package mapping and
explicit fallback list. The repository mirrors this in icons/bundled/catalog.json
and docs/icons/bundled-sources.md. All SVGs are self-contained local drawings.
Unsupported package aliases, absent upstream artwork, and loading/decode errors
use Butter Launcher's own Line icons, not artwork attributed to either upstream.
Unknown packages use the original Other app glyph.

The third-party licenses above cover their respective artwork. Importing these
assets does not select a license for Butter Launcher's own application source
or original Line icons. Brands and identifying designs remain subject to their
owners' trademark rights; inclusion does not imply endorsement or affiliation.
