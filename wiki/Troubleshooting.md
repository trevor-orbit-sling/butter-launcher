# Troubleshooting

## I cannot choose Butter as my home screen

Replay **Customise → Quick tour** and use **Home app settings**. Some manufacturers restrict changing the home app; you can still open Butter Launcher as an app.

## An app disappeared

Look in **Settings → Hidden apps** and select **Restore**. Hiding an app affects All apps, while an existing favourite stays available. Butter's own installations are hidden automatically. Uninstalled apps must be reinstalled before they can launch.

## An icon pack or preview is missing

Open **Customise → Icons → Choose icons**. The five included styles remain available without installed packs. Installed packs must support a compatible format. Previews use visible installed apps, so hiding every app can leave the preview area empty.

## Continue watching is empty

Android TV's native feed is device dependent. Standard AOSP providers normally restrict third-party launchers to their own entries, and Butter does not publish shows. Other TVs may share programs from participating apps. You can hide **Continue watching** in Customise.

## A remote button does not work

Use **Remote & controller** to learn an available shortcut. Home, Power and other reserved buttons stay with the system. A TV can intercept a button before Butter receives it.

## Weather is missing or fails to refresh

Choose a city through **Location**, check **Weather** is on, and confirm the TV has internet access. Weather is off on fresh installations. Use Down or the keyboard's Search action to move from city entry to search results.

## The screen saver setting is unavailable

The **Screen saver** shortcut falls back to general TV settings if necessary. Selection and idle timing depend on your TV; some devices do not expose a selectable screen saver list. Installing Butter does not enable it automatically.

A disabled manufacturer **Display → Screen** control is not Butter's screen saver selector. Butter cannot determine why that vendor control is disabled. Use an actual **Screen saver** or **Daydream** picker if the TV exposes one, and check that the selected screen saver is still installed.

## HDMI or another external input is missing

Open **Inputs** at the top of Home. Butter can show only external sources your TV exposes to apps. If the list is empty or a source cannot open, use **TV settings** or your remote's Input/Source button. The system may reserve that button, so assign another available button if you want a Butter Inputs shortcut.
